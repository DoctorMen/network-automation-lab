## Context

The three core routers are joined by Ethernet links (veth pairs) that only ever have two ends. On Ethernet, OSPF defaults to the broadcast network type, which elects a Designated Router. Both ends of a fresh link sit in the Waiting state for the full dead interval before electing one.

## Decision

Each router link is a /31 (RFC 3021) and is configured `ip ospf network point-to-point`. The addresses come from `links:` in `inventory/network.yml`, so the plan is written once.

Measured in two throwaway FRR 8.4.1 containers joined by a veth (2026-10-15, two runs each): point-to-point reached Full in 10 s both times, broadcast took 40 s and 41 s.

## Consequences

- No DR/BDR state to reason about, and adjacencies form within one hello interval.
- Half the address space of /30s.
- `netlab validate` treats /31 ends as usable addresses (no network or broadcast address check below /31).
- Anything that doesn't support /31s needs its link renumbered.
