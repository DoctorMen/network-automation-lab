## What no detection here sees

- Logins and commands outside FRR's CLI: UnexpectedAdmin reads only the routers' accounting proxies, so a shell login (RISK-61), root's own commands (RISK-60), and any login on fw1, h1, h2, noc or isp page nothing.
- What fw1 drops: each rule's packets are counted, and nothing logs or alerts on them, because LOG writes nothing from a container's network namespace (docs/controls.yml, fw1:default-drop).
- Traffic that never crosses fw1: r3 reaches h2's port 22 without it (tests/live/test_dataplane.py::test_firewall_blocks_ssh_to_servers proves that on every run), and host-to-host traffic inside LAN-A or LAN-B reaches no sensor.
- A tunnel inside ICMP, which fw1's allow-icmp forwards in both directions.
- Anything inside the carrier's network beyond the r2 to isp session.

## The false-positive review

Reviewed 2026-10-16 in the `sandbox` environment, on commit b4771ab: 30 minutes of the benign workload below, on a lab with nothing broken, so every page in that window is false. Pages are what the NOC's pager (the webhook sink) received from the workload's start until 120 s after its end, the longest a page it caused could take to arrive. The next review is due by 2027-01-16; `make test` fails after that, and for any detection whose rules have changed since. Run `make fp-review` on a full lab (`make deploy`).

The workload:

- every 30 s, a user on h1 loads h2's page
- every 10 s, h1 pings h2
- every 10 s, h1 pings the carrier's 203.0.113.1
- every 5 min, the operator runs make drift: a backup of every router and the firewall, compared with intent
- every 5 min, the operator runs make apply with nothing to change: the canary, its health gate, then the rest
- every 5 min, make verify: kernels, daemons and h2's QoS classes in effect
- every 5 min, make testevent: a marked line from every log source
- every 5 min, make locate from h1 to 203.0.113.1
